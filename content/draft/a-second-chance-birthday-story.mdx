9th April - my birthday.

Like most working days, it started normally. Office work, calls, and the usual routine. But the plan for the evening was simple - meet friends, eat good food, and just enjoy the day.

I work in IT Park, Chandigarh, and we decided to celebrate close by, around Sukhna Lake. Nothing too fancy - just good company and a peaceful vibe.

## A Small Moment That Stayed

While waiting outside New Panna Sweet, one of my friends got stuck on a call, so I just stood there watching people pass by. I’ve always loved trying different kinds of pastries, which is why this place is one of my go-to spots.

That’s when I noticed her.

A small girl - maybe 6 or 7 years old - going around asking people for food.

She wasn’t near me. She was on the other side of the road.

I kept looking at her. And honestly, I felt something inside - a simple thought:

- "I should get her something to eat."

Before I could act, a car passed.

And just like that - she was gone.

## The Feeling That Didn’t Leave

I waited.

5 minutes.  
10 minutes.  
Maybe more.

I looked around, hoping she would come back. But she didn’t.

And even though it was a small moment, it stayed in my mind.

- Why didn’t I call her?
- Why didn’t I just go to her?
- Why did I wait?

Sometimes we overthink simple things.

## Then Something Unexpected Happened

We went inside, ordered food, and started celebrating. Good food, laughter, everything was fine.

But that thought - it didn’t go away.

And then came the moment I can’t explain.

When we were at the billing counter - she appeared again.

The same girl.

This time - she came straight to me.

## No Planning - Just Action

This time, I didn’t wait.

I asked her what she wanted.

She said:

- chocolates
- ice cream
- and very specifically - laddus

That made me smile.

We got her chocolates and ice cream. Then she mentioned she had brothers and sisters.

So we didn’t stop there.

We bought 12 laddus.

Not because it was a big gesture - but because it felt right.

## A Simple Happiness

She was happy.

And honestly - so was I.

No big celebration, no expensive plan, nothing fancy matched that moment.

That small interaction made the whole day feel complete.

## Coincidence or Something More?

I don’t know what to call it.

- coincidence
- timing
- or something bigger

But it felt like life gave me a second chance.

A chance to act on a thought I almost ignored.

And maybe that’s the lesson.

## What I Took From That Day

Sometimes -

- small thoughts matter
- small actions matter more
- and timing... works in strange ways

You don’t always get a second chance.

But when you do - don’t hesitate.

## Ending Thought

That birthday wasn’t about the place, the food, or even the celebration.

It was about a moment.

A small, unexpected, human moment.

And I’m grateful for it.

---

- Sometimes, the best part of your day is not what you plan
- It’s what you feel

And this time - I felt it fully.
